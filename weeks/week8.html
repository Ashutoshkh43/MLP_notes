WEEK 8 · CONSOLIDATION

Integrated Revision & Model Improvement

The supplied portal shows a Week 8 section but its expanded lesson titles were not included in the provided outline. This page is deliberately framed as an integrated revision module, not a claim about the exact official lesson list.

SECTION 01

A reusable project recipe

  • Clarify the target and the evaluation metric.
  • Inspect rows, columns, types, missingness, class balance and potential leakage.
  • Split the data appropriately before fitting any data-dependent transformation.
  • Build a preprocessing pipeline and a simple baseline.
  • Compare a small set of justified model families with cross-validation.
  • Tune a limited set of meaningful hyperparameters, then evaluate once on the test set.
SECTION 02

Debugging poor performance

  • Check whether train and validation scores indicate underfitting or overfitting.
  • Verify target construction, data types, missing-value strategy, encoding and scaling.
  • Check for distribution shift or a split that does not match deployment.
  • Inspect residuals or misclassified examples to find systematic error patterns.
  • Prefer the simplest model that meets the objective unless complexity provides a demonstrated benefit.
SECTION 03

Mini-project deliverables

  • Problem statement and metric justification.
  • Data dictionary and concise exploratory analysis.
  • Leakage-aware preprocessing and a reproducible split.
  • Baseline plus model comparison table.
  • Error analysis, limitations and conclusion.
SECTION 04

Practice

  • Complete a small tabular ML workflow without copying preprocessing into separate train/test code paths.
  • Write a short explanation of why your selected model is suitable.
  • Prepare to defend your split, metric and hyperparameter choices in a viva.
